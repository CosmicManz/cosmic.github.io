Space Exploration

Humans have used rockets, spacecraft, and robots to learn more about space. Space Exploration benefits humanity as it helps us understand space and our own planet better. The most notable example are the Apollo Missions. Most notably Apollo 11, humans stepped foot on the surface of the Moon for the first time. The International Space Station has been used by many nations for scientific research. NASA has used robots to explore Mars.
